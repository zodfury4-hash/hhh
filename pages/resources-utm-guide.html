1. The 5 Standard UTM Parameters Explained

UTM parameters are the industry-standard mechanism for marketing attribution. When a user clicks a tagged link, Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Mixpanel, and backend data warehouses read these parameters to map user acquisition accurately.

Parameter Requirement Purpose Standard Examples
utm_source Required Identifies the platform, search engine, or vendor sending traffic. google, facebook, instagram, newsletter
utm_medium Required Describes the top-level marketing medium or distribution mechanism. cpc, paid_social, email, affiliate, organic
utm_campaign Required The specific product promotion, seasonal sale, or strategic launch. summer-launch-2026, black-friday-early-access
utm_term Optional Used in search ads for paid keywords or targeting audience segments. running-shoes, saas-crm-software
utm_content Optional Differentiates ads or links pointing to the same destination for A/B testing. banner-hero-v1, cta-blue-button, carousel-slide-2

2. GA4 Attribution Standards & Common Pitfalls

⚠️ The Case-Sensitivity Trap: Google Analytics 4 is strictly case-sensitive. Tagging traffic with utm_source=Facebook, utm_source=facebook, and utm_source=FB will create three separate rows in your traffic acquisition reports. Always normalize to lowercase.

Golden Rules for Bulletproof UTM Tags

  • Enforce Lowercase: Always convert all parameter values to lowercase prior to deployment.
  • Use Hyphens for Spaces: Never include spaces. Spaces encode to %20 or +, which degrades readability and causes parsing glitches across email clients.
  • Preserve Existing Query Strings: If your target URL already has parameters (e.g. ?variant=blue&ref=123), UTM tags must be merged using & rather than a second ?.
  • Place Query Strings Before Hashes: Fragment identifiers (#hero, #pricing) must remain at the very end of the URL. Placing UTMs after a hash prevents servers from reading them.
  • Never Double-Encode: Parameters should be encoded once using standard URL components.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

What are UTM parameters and why are they important? ▼
UTM (Urchin Tracking Module) parameters are five standardized query tags appended to destination URLs to inform web analytics systems (like GA4) where incoming visitors arrived from, what campaign prompted their visit, and which ad creative was clicked.
Why does character casing matter in Google Analytics 4? ▼
Google Analytics 4 is strictly case-sensitive. Tagging URLs with 'Facebook', 'facebook', and 'FACEBOOK' creates three independent source buckets, fragmenting your reports. Standardizing on lowercase prevents split metrics.
Does HEA Link support dynamic UTM parameters? ▼
Yes. You can pass dynamic macros (such as Google Ads {keyword} or Meta {{campaign.name}}) into your HEA Link destination, and our redirect engine safely forwards them to the destination page.
Can I track UTM parameters inside the HEA Link dashboard? ▼
Yes. In addition to passing them to GA4, HEA Link aggregates click performance by Campaign, Source, and Medium directly inside the Analytics and Campaigns views of your dashboard.
