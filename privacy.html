Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

HEA Link ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy and is dedicated to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, and safeguard information when you use the HEA Link URL shortener, vector QR code generator, and link-in-bio platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

  • Account Information: When you register for an account, we collect your name, email address, and hashed authentication credentials.
  • Link and Telemetry Data: When short links are visited, our servers collect aggregate diagnostic data including timestamp, user-agent (browser and operating system family), referrer source, and coarse geographical country/city. We do not store raw IP addresses in permanent analytics logs.
  • Payment Details: Upgrades to PRO plans are processed securely by Stripe. We never store credit card numbers or banking secrets on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is utilized exclusively for:

  • Providing fast and reliable URL redirection, dynamic vector QR code generation, and branded bio pages.
  • Generating real-time analytics graphs and campaign performance telemetry for your links.
  • Detecting, preventing, and taking down malicious URLs (phishing, malware, SSRF exploits, and scam links).
  • Sending transaction notifications, security alerts, and email verification codes.

3. Cookies and Tracking

HEA Link uses essential session tokens (JWT) to keep you authenticated. We do not sell user data to advertising networks or third-party data brokers.

4. Security Measures

All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS with TLS 1.3. User passwords are salted and hashed using bcrypt. Our API endpoints are protected by rate limiting, SSRF filters, Content Security Policy headers, and Google Safe Browsing heuristics.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

You have the right to inspect, export, or delete your account and associated links at any time from your settings panel. To submit a data deletion request, contact privacy@healink.store.
