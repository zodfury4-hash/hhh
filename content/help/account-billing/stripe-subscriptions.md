# Managing Your Stripe Billing and Invoices

All subscription billing, payment methods, and tax receipts are managed through our PCI-DSS Level 1 certified Stripe integration.

## Updating Your Payment Method

1. Go to **Dashboard → Settings** (`#settings`).
2. Select the **Billing** tab.
3. Click **Manage Billing in Stripe Portal**.
4. Add, update, or remove credit cards, debit cards, or Apple Pay payment methods.

## Downloading Invoices and Receipts
Historical invoices and PDF payment receipts are available for download 24/7 inside the Stripe customer portal. Each invoice includes your business VAT / GST identification number if configured.

## Canceling Your Subscription
You can cancel your subscription at any time without talking to a sales agent:
- Click **Cancel Plan** inside the Stripe Customer Portal.
- Your Pro benefits remain active until the end of your current billing cycle.
- Your existing short links, custom domains, and redirect traffic will never be broken or deleted.
