# Understanding Plan Limits and Account Tiers

HEA Link offers flexible tiers tailored for individual creators, fast-growing startups, and high-volume enterprise organizations.

## Feature Breakdown by Tier

| Feature | Free Tier | Pro Tier | Enterprise Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monthly Short Links** | 1,000 / month | 25,000 / month | Unlimited |
| **Custom Back-Halves** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Vector QR Codes** | Unlimited | Unlimited with Logos | Unlimited Vector SVG |
| **Custom Domains** | 1 Domain | 5 Domains | Unlimited Domains |
| **Password Protection** | Included | Included | Included |
| **Link Expiration & Limits** | Included | Included | Included |
| **Developer REST API** | 60 req/min | 600 req/min | 6,000+ req/min |
| **Outbound Webhooks** | 1 Webhook | 10 Webhooks | Unlimited Webhooks |
| **Click Telemetry Retention** | 400 Days | 400 Days | Custom / Indefinite |
| **SLA Uptime Guarantee** | Best-effort | 99.9% Uptime | 99.99% Guaranteed SLA |

## Upgrading Your Plan
You can upgrade to Pro or Enterprise directly within **Settings → Subscription** using any major credit or debit card processed securely through Stripe.
