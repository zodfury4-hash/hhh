# How to Bulk-Shorten Multiple URLs

Marketing teams and content managers frequently need to shorten large batches of links for multi-product catalogs, SMS broadcast campaigns, or print collaterals.

## Submitting a Batch

1. On the homepage or in the dashboard, select the **Bulk Shorten** tab.
2. Paste your list of URLs into the multi-line text area (one full URL per line, starting with `http://` or `https://`).
3. You can submit up to 20 URLs per batch submission.
4. Click **Bulk Shorten Links →**.

## Batch Validation & Result Table

- **Validation:** Invalid or malformed URLs are clearly flagged without rejecting valid URLs in the batch.
- **Immediate Generation:** Each link is assigned a unique crypto-random slug.
- **Copy & Export:** You can copy individual short links directly from the results table or click **Copy All** to paste the complete mapping into your spreadsheet or CRM.
