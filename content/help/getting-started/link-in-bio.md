# Setting Up Your Link-in-Bio Mobile Page

A Link-in-Bio page provides creators, influencers, and businesses with a centralized microsite to direct followers from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and X bios.

## Building Your Bio Page

1. Open **Pages** (`#pages`) in your dashboard.
2. Click **Create Bio Page**.
3. Set your username handle (e.g. `healink.store/bio/sarahcreative`).

## Customizing Your Profile
- **Avatar & Bio:** Upload an avatar image and write a compelling 160-character bio headline.
- **Link Blocks:** Add interactive button cards with custom icons, titles, and target destination URLs.
- **Social Icons:** Link your verified accounts across YouTube, Instagram, GitHub, LinkedIn, Spotify, and TikTok.
- **Themes & Aesthetics:** Choose between clean minimalist, glassmorphic dark, and neon gradient palettes.

## Tracking Engagement
Each button click on your bio page is recorded in your dashboard analytics. You can see which links generate the most visitor interest, broken down by country, referrer, and device.
