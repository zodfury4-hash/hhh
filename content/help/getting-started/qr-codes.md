# Creating and Customizing Scannable QR Codes

Every short link on HEA Link can be paired with an interactive 2D QR code optimized for smartphones, conference badges, retail menus, and print ads.

## Generating a QR Code

1. In your dashboard, open the **QR Studio** (`#qr`).
2. Enter your destination URL, Wi-Fi credentials, or text snippet.
3. Select an active short link to link scan metrics directly to your link telemetry.

## Color Styling & Contrast

- **Foreground Color:** Choose from preset neon and brand shades (`#0070f3`, `#00d2ff`, `#ff007f`, `#0e1a33`), or enter a custom hex color code.
- **Background Contrast:** For maximum scanner compatibility, always maintain high contrast between the code dark modules and background light modules.
- **Quiet Zone:** HEA Link automatically applies a 4-module quiet zone margin to prevent scanning interference.

## Export Formats
- **PNG (Raster):** Standard 500x500 or 1000x1000 px raster image for web embeds and digital presentations.
- **SVG (Vector):** Infinite vector scalability for billboards, physical product packaging, and large-format print media without pixelation.
